## 3. CORE LOOP ✅

**Daily Moments + Cooldowns**

**Moments (soft bonuses):**
- Morning (7-10 AM): +50% bond from feeding
- Afternoon (12-2 PM): +25% XP
- Evening (6-9 PM): +50% bond from feeding

**Cooldowns:**
- 30-min digestion after feeding
- Can still feed at 25% value
- Timer visible

## 6. PET UNLOCKS ✅

| Type | Pets | How |
|------|------|-----|
| **Free** | Munchlet, Grib, Plompo | Start with all 3 |
| **Earnable** | Fizz | Bond Level 5 (any pet) |
| **Earnable** | Ember | 10 mini-games completed |
| **Premium** | Chomper, Whisp, Luxe | Sub, purchase, OR late achievements |

### Earnable Pet Details

| Pet | Achievement | Rationale |
|-----|-------------|-----------|
| **Fizz** 🔵 | Reach Bond Level 5 with any pet | Rewards caring for your pet (relationship) |
| **Ember** 🟠 | Complete 10 mini-games (any combination) | Rewards playing with your pet (activities) |

### Why These Achievements?

**Fizz = Bond Level 5**
- Bond = relationship, not XP grinding
- You "discover" Fizz by caring, not checking boxes
- Bond grows naturally through regular care
- Aligns with "Personality over stats" pillar

**Ember = 10 Mini-games**
- Mini-games = fun activities with your pet
- You "discover" Ember through play variety
- With energy limits (5 games/day max), takes 2+ days minimum
- Encourages return visits, not farming
- Aligns with "Rhythm over grind" pillar

## 13. MINI-GAME REWARDS ✅

**Energy-limited. Conservative.**

| Tier | Coins | Gem | Food |
|------|-------|-----|------|
| Bronze | 3 | — | — |
| Silver | 7 | — | 40% common |
| Gold | 15 | — | 75% any |
| Rainbow | 22 | 1 | Rare |

**Energy:** 50 max, 10/game, 1 per 30 min regen. First daily game free.

**Per-game bonus:** +0.3 bond, +7 happiness (playing with your pet!)

**Fizz ability:** +25% COINS only (not gems, not bond)

---

## 14. CLASSIC CONSEQUENCES ✅

**Runaway + Evolution Corruption. NO permadeath.**

**Neglect path:**
1. Pet sad, warns you
2. Pet sick
3. Pet threatens to leave
4. **Pet runs away** (48h lockout)

**Return:** Wait 48h OR 25 gems. Bond -50%. Rebuild trust.

**Corruption:** Poor care = "Survivor" evolution variant. Healable with 30 days good care.
